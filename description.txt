Skill Bar adds a draggable 10-slot bar for quick access to items without cluttering your main hotbar.

Slots are bookmarks only — items stay in your inventory. Press a bound key or click a slot to use that item toward your cursor, just like the normal hotbar.

SUPPORTED ITEMS
• Weapons, spells, and summons (vanilla-accurate shooting)
• Pickaxes, axes, and hammers
• Blocks, platforms, rope, and walls
• Potions, food, and buff drinks (consumes from inventory; slot clears when empty)
• Magic Mirror, Cell Phone, boss summons, and similar usable items

CONTROLS
• Assign: left-click a slot with an item, or Shift+click from inventory
• Use: Z X C V B N M , . / by default (configurable), middle-click, or Alt+click
• Move bar: drag the hand icon on the left
• Clear slot: right-click
• Copy bookmark: Shift+click a filled slot

Configure keys and options under Mod Configuration → Skill Bar. Bar position and bookmarks save per character. Client-side UI; works in multiplayer.

Default slot 1 key: Z
