[h1]Item Bar[/h1]

[b]A second hotbar for Terraria — bookmark items from your inventory and use them with dedicated keys, aimed at your cursor.[/b]

Item Bar adds a draggable 10-slot bar so you can reach for tools, spells, potions, and more without swapping your main hotbar selection. Slots are [b]bookmarks only[/b]: items stay in your inventory.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Early beta[/h2]

[b]This mod is in very early beta — and my first ever mod![/b] Please be gentle if something breaks :) Some items may behave oddly or not work at all — especially [b]melee weapons[/b], which often fail from the item bar. That is not the main intention of this mod; it is meant for quick access to summons, spells, tools, consumables, mirrors, and similar items.

[b]Not tested with controller / gamepad input[/b] — keyboard and mouse only for now.

If something breaks, please report it on GitHub (link below).

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Why use Item Bar?[/h2]

The main goal is quality-of-life for item switching. For example, if you are not a summoner but still keep a summon for help, you normally select the summon staff, use it, then switch back to your weapon.

With Item Bar, press one key — the summon is used toward your cursor while your hotbar selection stays unchanged.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Supported items[/h2]
Mods note: Should be compatible with any mod item really but obviously can't test all of it. Give it a go, chances are it will just be fine :)
[list]
[*]Weapons, spells, and summons (vanilla-accurate shooting)
[*]Pickaxes, axes, and hammers
[*]Potions, food, and buff drinks (consumes from inventory; slot clears when empty)
[*]Magic Mirror, Cell Phone, boss summons, and similar usable items
[/list]

[h2]Not supported / known issues[/h2]

[list]
[*]Yoyos and flails / maces (Sunfury, etc.) — use your hotbar for those weapons
[*][b]Melee weapons[/b] — often do not work; melee combat is not this mod's main focus
[*][b]Controller / gamepad[/b] — not tested; use keyboard and mouse
[*]Most blocks, platforms, rope, and walls don't work, not intended either
[*]Channel weapons (flamethrowers, drills) also not supported
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Controls[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Assign:[/b] left-click a slot while holding or hovering an item
[*][b]Use:[/b] Z X C V B N M , . / by default (configurable)
[*][b]Move bar:[/b] drag the hand icon on the left
[*][b]Clear slot:[/b] right-click
[/list]

Configure keys under [b]Mod Configuration → Item Bar[/b] or [b]Settings → Key Bindings → Item Bar[/b]. Bar position and bookmarks save per character.

Default slot 1 key: [b]Z[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]

Item Bar UI and keybinds are client-side. Each player sets up their own bar. Works in multiplayer.
